The first release of Tabsome: tabs for any window, from any app.

- Drag a window over the middle of another one, pause, and let go: they become tabs of one window.
- Tabs work like browser tabs: click to switch, × or middle-click to close, drag to reorder, and drag one out to make it its own window again. Double-click a tab to rename it.
- Tabs along the top or down the left side, in three sizes, with app badges and hover previews.
- Keyboard shortcuts in the browser layout, with ⌃⌥ in place of ⌘ (⌃⌥Tab, ⌃⌥1–9, ⌃⌥W), all rebindable.
- Quitting Tabsome never closes a window: every group comes apart, and each window is left on its own.

Requires macOS 26 or later. Tabsome needs the Accessibility permission to move windows. Screen Recording is optional and only used for window previews and animations.
